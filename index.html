About IDIP

Industrial Decision Intelligence Platform

Project Purpose
IDIP is an AI-powered predictive maintenance platform designed to evaluate industrial machine operating conditions and estimate machine failure risk.

The platform transforms machine sensor data into actionable maintenance intelligence by combining machine learning, risk classification and maintenance recommendations.
